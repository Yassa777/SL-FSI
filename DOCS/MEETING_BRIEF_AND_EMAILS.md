## One-Page Meeting Brief (Share in advance)

### 1) Project Summary (30 seconds)
We are building a Sri Lanka Financial Stress Index and regime timeline using official data. The model is descriptive, not predictive. Our goal is to align the timeline with policy realities and upgrade data quality (reserves, FX, banking stress, debt yields). We want guidance on the right indicators and definitions, and we want to validate our timeline against internal views of stress periods.

### 2) Why We Need You
- Our current dataset relies on public series, some monthly and some interpolated.
- Stress signals can be distorted without institutional context (reserve usability, FX policy shifts, liquidity pressure).
- Your feedback will help make the regime timeline more accurate and useful.

### 3) What We Will Share
- A draft stress timeline (CALM / STRESS / CRISIS) for 2020-2024.
- A short list of core indicators and their definitions.
- A list of data gaps that limit our inference.

### 4) Key Questions (All Departments)
- Which months in 2021-2023 would you label as STRESS vs CRISIS?
- Which indicators are leading vs lagging for Sri Lanka?
- Which series are most reliable and least revised?
- Are there internal series or proxies we should use instead?

### 5) Deliverable Back to You
- A short internal note summarizing timeline alignment and data corrections.
- A revised, documented indicator set with clear definitions and caveats.
